# partnership-dashboard-demo

This folder is a layout example. Every customer, hub, leader, cost and volume in it is dummy data. Do not treat any figure as live performance.

The page is a weekly sales view for a retail partnership. It puts orders, quotes and referral activity on one screen, by location, leader and region, so a review does not depend on separate spreadsheets.

## Who it is for

Sales leaders, and the analyst who prepares the weekly partnership review.

## What it answers

- How did orders, quotes, open quotes, conversion and cancellations move last week versus the week before, and year to date?
- Which locations are driving referral visits, assisted leads and assisted orders?
- Where should a leader focus this week (hand-off, low referral use, weak conversion, or a quiet week)?
- Which regions are carrying quotes and orders?
- What does the product mix look like, and how do order and quote statuses split?
- How does one location or leader compare with the rest?

## The problem it solved

Orders, quotes and referral visits lived in different extracts. A leader could not see, in one place, whether a site was quiet, converting, or getting visits that never became leads. The dashboard turns that into a week-on-week matrix, a short opportunity list, a store leaderboard and a region map.
